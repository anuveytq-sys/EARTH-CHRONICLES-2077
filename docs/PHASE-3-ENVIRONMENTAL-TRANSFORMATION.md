# Phase 3 — Environmental Transformation

Phase 3 turns Earth Chronicles 2077 from a timeline viewer into an interactive environmental restoration strategy simulator.

## Restoration Lab
Open **Restoration Lab** from the left navigation rail. The lab includes six independently adjustable programs:
- Forest corridors — forest protection and habitat connectivity
- Wetland recovery — floodplain and peatland restoration
- Dryland regeneration — soil cover and water retention
- Habitat rewilding — connected native habitats
- Coastal and reef protection — mangrove, seagrass and reef stewardship
- Urban nature networks — shade, green roofs and permeable public space

Each program has a 0–100 allocation control, explanatory context, and live-updating gameplay indicators.

## Ecosystem views
Filter the program list by all systems, land, freshwater, ocean, and cities.

## Strategy presets
- Balanced Recovery
- Biodiversity First
- Water Security
- Blue Planet

Presets provide starting allocations; every allocation remains editable. Reset Allocation returns to the initial strategy.

## Simulation indicators
The lab recalculates restoration momentum, habitat connectivity, water resilience, coastal health, and urban greening as allocations change. Applying a strategy updates the visible regional markers and their restoration halos on the 3D globe, and shifts the planetary restoration signal.

## Scope and scientific integrity
The scores are transparent gameplay indices derived from the chosen allocations. They are not measured ecological outcomes, satellite observations, live NDVI, or validated forecasts. Real restoration outcomes depend on local ecology, governance, land rights, climate, time horizons, and ongoing monitoring. The simulation is intended for exploration and learning, not operational environmental planning.

## Compatibility
Phase 3 is additive: it keeps the existing Three.js globe, region selection, settings, and Phase 2 timeline engine. The restoration lab is an overlay and does not replace the temporal engine.
